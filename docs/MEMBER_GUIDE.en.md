# 👤 Member guide

> You only need your own progress; leaders handle the rest.

## After you sign in
- **📊 My progress**: your completion only
  - Green bar = done
  - Tap a badge, then a segment, then an item
  - ⓘ on the right shows assessment notes from the 11th Edition scheme (English when EN is on)
  - **You cannot tick**: use “📝 Claim”

- **⭐ Other badges**: at the bottom of My progress (First Aid, AYP, lifesaving, etc.)

- **📝 Approvals**: whether your claims were approved (⏳ awaiting leader / ✅ approved)

- **📅 Activity log** (v8.1): your **service** (with hours), **activities**, and **courses** (with certificate numbers). Leaders enter them; you are read-only.

- **🖨️ Print forms**: PT/19 (Venture Scout Award) and PT/20 (Dragon Scout Award) dates filled from progress. Edit address / District / Region, then print double-sided.

- **📚 Library**: Safe from Harm, Training Scheme, wearing guide

- **❓ Guide**: this file, filtered by role

Tap **中 / EN** (top right) to switch the whole app. Item IDs and cloud records stay the same — only the display language changes.

## How to claim an item
1. Find it in My progress
2. Tap 📝 Claim
3. Date + evidence (e.g. 3 photos from the 2026-05-01 camp)
4. Submit — drafted in the browser; a leader sees it under Approvals
5. When approved, progress updates

### Why can’t I see other members?
Privacy by default. The GSL can turn on “Allow members to view one another’s progress” under Users → settings.

### Forgot password? / How do I get an account?
- On the sign-in page tap **“🆕 Apply for an account (member / exec / leader)”**, pick a role, enter YMIS, name and email (required for exec / leader).
- **Do not fill in section / unit**: section = Venture Scouts, unit = the Group you picked.
- A GSL / VSL approves it under Approvals → Accounts and issues a one-time temporary password. You must change it at first sign-in.
- GSL / admin roles are created by the current GSL under Users.
- You may also ask a leader to reset a temporary password under Users.
